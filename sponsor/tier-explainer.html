Step 2 — Two doors into every tier

EXCLUSIVE

One brand owns the tier's placements for the whole term. Nobody else appears there. The strongest pitch: "your logo IS there."

Pays the full tier rate, upfront.

SHARED ROTATION

Up to 4 sponsors split the tier's placements. Each one owns it for a full week — static all week, swaps Monday morning. Never a carousel, never animated: one sponsor per screen, period.

Pays ≈ ⅓ of the tier rate, upfront. Tier 4 never rotates.

Step 4 — The rotation rules (all of them)

  • Payment order = queue order. Whoever pays first gets first pick of weeks; everyone after lines up behind.
  • Rotation starts immediately. No waiting for four sponsors. Two in? They alternate. The placement never sits empty.
  • Monday swaps, static all week. Your logo is the only one on that screen Monday through Sunday. Monday morning it flips to the next sponsor.
  • Max 4 per rotation. A fifth payer starts a waitlist.
  • Expired terms backfill from the waitlist. When someone's term ends, the next payer in line slides into their week slot. Renewing sponsors get first dibs on keeping their slot (peak months go to renewers first).
  • No competitor exclusions — rotations are first-paid, first-served, period. They're all competitors; if someone has a genuine problem sharing a rotation, they can raise it and Tanner deals with it then.
  • Tanner doesn't run the queue. The waitlist, the schedule, the Monday swaps, the backfill — all of it runs without him. He cashes the checks and only steps in if there's a dispute.

3. FAQ

Questions prospects will ask

When does my ad go live?

As soon as you've paid. You're added to the rotation queue in payment order and you're live at the next Monday swap — at the latest. No waiting for the rotation to fill.

What if only two sponsors sign up — do I still pay full price?

Yes, the shared price is the shared price. But with two in, you each get every other week — that's more exposure per dollar than a full four-way rotation, not less.

What happens when my term expires?

Your week slot goes to the next payer on the waitlist. If you want to keep it, renew — renewing sponsors get first dibs, and peak months (Oct–Jan) are offered to renewers before anyone else.

Can I upgrade from shared to exclusive mid-term?SETTLED 2026-10-04

Upgrades take effect at renewal, not mid-term — and you get first dibs on buying your tier outright before the slot opens to anyone else. Nobody who's paid gets bumped mid-term, ever. One exception: if you're the only paid sponsor in a rotation, there's nobody to displace — you can upgrade on the spot.

If someone in my rotation goes exclusive, what happens to me?

Nothing mid-term — upgrades only happen at renewal, so your paid term runs its full course. At renewal, if a rotation-mate buys the tier exclusively, your slot ends and you can join the waitlist for another tier or take the exclusive tier yourself next cycle. No pro-rated refunds needed because nobody's term gets cut short.

How is an upgrade priced — do I get credit for what I already paid?

At renewal there's nothing to credit — your old term is done, and the exclusive term starts fresh at the exclusive price (founding rate if you're a founding sponsor). For the single-sponsor on-the-spot exception, the unused months of your shared payment are credited toward the exclusive price, and the new 6- or 12-month term starts that day.

What if my competitor joins my rotation?

Rotations are first-paid, first-served — there's no competitor exclusion. They're all competitors. If you have a genuine problem with someone in your rotation, raise it and Tanner sorts it out case by case.

Who do I contact about my ad — placements, changes, problems?

Day-to-day, the queue runs itself — schedule, Monday swaps, backfills, all automatic. If something needs a human decision, it goes to Tanner.

Do I get any reporting?SETTLED 2026-10-04

Yes — monthly reporting from public launch, built on App Store and Google Play numbers: downloads, active users, sessions. "Your placements were live in front of X trappers this month." That's the whole of it — no click or tap reporting, no website-traffic numbers. And no in-app tracking, ever — the app stays no-account, no-cloud, nothing phoning home. During beta, reporting is what's verifiable: tester count, your placements live, screenshots of your branding in the app.

Can I run a shorter trial before committing to 6 or 12 months?SETTLED 2026-10-04

No. Terms are 6 or 12 months, paid upfront — no trials, no exceptions. The 6-month term at $210 (Tier 1 shared) is already the low-risk way in: one season, then decide.
