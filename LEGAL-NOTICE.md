## About the Information in This App

Opossum Foot is a pocket notebook and reference tool. It is not a law book, not a
field guide, and not a substitute for either.

**Regulations and seasons.** The season dates, zones, limits, and regulation notes
in this app are summaries prepared for quick reference — the same way the printed
regulations booklet in your truck is a summary. They are not complete, they are
not the law, and they can be wrong: agencies set and change seasons every year,
sometimes mid-season, and a summary can lag behind or miss a change. The official
publications and websites of each state's fish and wildlife agency, and the state
code itself, are the only authorities. Before you set a trap, hunt, or travel to
trap somewhere new, verify the current rules with that state's agency. A season
reminder or an "open" flag in this app is a nudge to check — it is never
permission. You are solely responsible for knowing and obeying the laws and
regulations everywhere you trap, and for holding the licenses, permits, and tags
those laws require.

Opossum Foot carries the most recent regulations and rules each state has
published. States publish on their own schedules, and some publish later
than others. Where a state's current guide has not yet been published, the
app shows the latest information that state has released and marks it as
such — when the state publishes, the app is updated. The state's current
official publication is always the authority.

**Sign, plants, and fungi.** The sign and forage information in this app is
educational, and it is written for identification by sight. It is not a foraging
guide. Identifications can be wrong — including any you record yourself with the
app's markers — and some wild plants and fungi have poisonous look-alikes. Never
eat, use, or feed to any person or animal any wild plant or fungus based on
anything in this app. Confirm any identification in person with a qualified local
expert.

**Maps, GPS, and land.** Maps, satellite imagery, GPS positions, routes, and any
boundary or landowner information in this app are approximate. They may be out of
date, misaligned, or wrong, and they are not a legal survey. Nothing in this app
gives you the right to enter any land. Obey posted signs, and get the landowner's
permission before you set foot — let alone a trap — on private ground. You are
responsible for knowing whose land you are on at all times.
